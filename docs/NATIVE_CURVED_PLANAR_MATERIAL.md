# Curved planar document material

The prepared native material/reference owner query now admits an additional family: axis-aligned physical Plane supports whose closed trims consist of actual document Straight and three-point Arc edges. Full circles are closed collections of open Arcs. The existing general straight Plane census is unchanged. Production horizontal plate/cylinder intersections, including authored holes, are covered when their actual prepared boundaries satisfy the proof.

The document Arc is the exact rational circumcircle through its three captured binary points, with the directed span selected by its via point. This proof does not replace that curve with the producer's analytic conic, a captured float/libm evaluator, or a chord polygon. Exact common physical Plane support is checked for every descendant.

Every source and child outer loop is certified Jordan. Exact line-line, line-circle and circle-circle intersections are computed with rational and quadratic-surd coordinates; only the prescribed adjacent endpoint contacts are allowed. Hole boundaries must be disjoint, strictly inside the outer loop, and mutually disjoint and non-nested. After those global predicates, an exact generic-ray winding calculation determines nesting. It does not select a sampled material witness. Conservative whole-curve bounds filter pair candidates. Every active-list scan, including a rejection, and every generic-ray vertex/tangency rejection scan consumes the shared request budget and cancellation checks. Dense bounding-box overlap still has quadratic worst-case work, but cannot hide that work from the budget. No measured speed claim is made.

Each Jordan loop is normalized to positive outer and negative hole winding independently of its stored traversal. The sign is proved by an outward interval Green integral. For a circular span it is `cross(C,Q-P) + R�*theta`; line spans use `cross(P,Q)`. This integral is solely an orientation certificate. Equal area cannot establish material equality.

Common exact line and circle carriers are grouped and split at every endpoint ray. Directed span endpoints create signed event deltas; an ordered sweep certifies zero child-minus-source boundary count on every atomic span. Circle rays use exact cross/dot ordering across a relative cut. Combined with certified positive Jordan domains, winding linearity gives `sum(1_Dchild)=1_Dsource` off the boundary. Positive normalization makes duplicated domains add multiplicity even when stored loops have opposite directions. Missing holes, missing children, duplicate children, and unmatched changed carriers refuse.

The proof shares the owner's aggregate configurable work budget, cache, detached source/current snapshots and final callback-free binding checks. It preserves cancellation exception identity and issues no accepted partial receipt on exhaustion. Existing geometry-owned reference and physical orientation checks remain independent. Domain evidence names the physical coordinate chart and the winding/atomic-span proof; endpoint lists in evidence are descriptive topology, not polygon truth.

Tilted curved planes currently refuse explicitly. They need a certified plane projection that retains the general conic representation, or an exact rational physical isometry. Elliptic, spline, quadric and other trim families need exact span membership/intersection predicates and directed carrier atomization before extension. Exterior Arc refits whose exact stored circumcircles differ from their source refuse literal material equality; a separately adopted whole-domain discrepancy proof is required. Shared actual internal joint curves can cancel without assuming they equal the producer's sampled analytic conic.

No float/libm preservation, opaque payload interpretation, consumer-owned external reference inventory, composed coordinate-error bound, or mesh permission is promoted. The public result remains a qualified document-material receipt within the admitted family, not full mesher admission.
